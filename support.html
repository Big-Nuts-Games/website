SUPPORT

Welcome to the Big Nuts Games Support page! We’re here to help you with any issues or questions about "The Test of Insanity" or other services we provide. Below, you’ll find answers to common questions and instructions on how to reach us.

1. How to Contact Us

If you need assistance, please email us at bignutsgames@gmail.com. Include the following details to help us assist you faster:
  • Your device type (e.g., iPhone 14, Samsung Galaxy S23)
  • Operating system version (e.g., iOS 17, Android 14)
  • A brief description of your issue
  • Screenshots, if applicable
We aim to respond within 48 hours, though it may take longer during busy periods.

2. Technical Issues

Having trouble with "The Test of Insanity"? Try these steps before contacting us:
  • Restart the App: Close and reopen the app.
  • Check Your Connection: Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
  • Update the App: Visit your app store to download the latest version.
  • Restart Your Device: Turn your device off and back on.
If the problem persists, reach out to us with the details listed in Section 1.

3. In-App Purchases

For issues with in-app purchases in "The Test of Insanity":
  • Purchase Not Showing Up? Wait a few minutes, then restart the app. If it still doesn’t appear, contact us.
  • Refund Requests: All purchases are handled by the app store (e.g., Apple App Store, Google Play Store). Please refer to their refund policies and submit requests directly through them.
  • Accidental Purchase? Contact the app store support team as soon as possible.
For further help, email us with your purchase receipt or transaction ID.

4. Account Help

Need assistance with your account?
  • Lost Progress: If your game progress isn’t saved, let us know your device details and when the issue started.
  • Account Issues: For login problems or suspected unauthorized access, email us immediately.
Note: We don’t currently support account transfers between devices, but we’ll do our best to assist with recovery.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why does the game crash?
A: Crashes can occur due to outdated software or low device memory. Update your app and device, and clear some storage space.

Q: Can I play offline?
A: Some features require an internet connection, but basic gameplay may work offline. Check your connection if you encounter issues.

Q: How do I report a bug?
A: Email us at bignutsgames@gmail.com with a description of the bug and any screenshots.
